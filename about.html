🛡️ Cyber Security Awareness Project

This project aims to spread awareness about cyber security and cyber crime among students. We conducted real sessions in schools to educate students about online safety and threats.

What We Did

🎯

Objectives

  • Create cyber awareness
  • Educate about online threats
  • Promote safe internet use
  • Reduce cyber risks
⚙️

Methodology

  • School awareness sessions
  • Real-life examples
  • Interactive teaching
  • Video demonstrations
📊

Outcomes

  • Improved awareness
  • Better online safety
  • Understanding of scams
  • Responsible behavior
