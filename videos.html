🛡️ Quick Safety Tips

🔒

Never share your OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank.

📵

Disconnect suspicious video calls immediately. Real police don't arrest digitally.

🔗

Never click unknown links received via SMS, WhatsApp, or email.

💰

Never send money to strangers online, no matter how convincing they sound.
