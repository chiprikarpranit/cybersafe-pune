🏫 Awareness Session at Prerna School

We successfully conducted a Cyber Security and Cyber Crime Awareness Session at Prerna School, Ambegaon Pathar, Pune for students of 8th, 9th, and 10th standards.

About the Session

Our team visited the school and educated students about online safety and cyber threats through interactive teaching, real-life examples, and video demonstrations.

Students actively participated and showed great interest in learning how to stay safe online. The session was well received by both students and school staff.
